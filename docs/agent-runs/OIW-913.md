## Architectural decisions
- Pacing is scaled to the tour panel's text length (3 to 6 s reading beat) rather than fixed 2-3 s beats, because the panel bodies run 30 to 70 words and a first-time viewer cannot read them in 2 s. This puts each walkthrough at about 128 s, inside the 60-150 s bound. Captions (VTT) carry the short plain-language summary.
- The cursor glide is animated inside the page and the real mouse is then moved to the same spot. Playwright's `slowMo` would otherwise add 120 ms to every intermediate move and make glides choppy.
- The spec imports `getTourSteps` from `apps/web/src/lib/tour/steps.ts` (read only) so the recording follows the shipped tour order and uses its real targets; it does not hard-code step selectors or titles.
- Captions in `presenter.ts` are pack-neutral wording keyed by tour step id.
- Marks are wall-clock seconds from page creation; the encoder trims about 0.5 s of browser start-up and shifts caption times to match.
- No ADR needed.
