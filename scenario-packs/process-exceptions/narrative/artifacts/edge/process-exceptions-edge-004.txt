Subject: B-2190 root cause — closed out, not supplier-related

Owen,

Following up on the viscosity deviation we saw on B-2190 back on the 5th. Maintenance found that the mixer 1 temperature probe had drifted out of calibration, which was throwing the viscosity reading off during the back half of that batch — nothing to do with the Kestrel Materials Co. binder lot (KM-LOT-448) that batch was run on. Probe's been recalibrated and verified against the reference standard as of yesterday.

Want to be clear on this one since it's easy to jump to "bad raw material lot" whenever we see a viscosity flag: this specific deviation on B-2190 was ruled out as supplier-related. Please don't carry it forward as evidence against KM-LOT-448 in any future write-up — the two aren't connected, at least not based on what we found here.
