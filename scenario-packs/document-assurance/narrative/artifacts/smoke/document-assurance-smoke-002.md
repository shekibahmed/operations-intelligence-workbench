INTERNAL POLICY DOCUMENT

Data Retention Policy
Policy No. POL-RET-014
Version 3.1
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Owner: Review Operations

1. PURPOSE

This policy establishes standard retention periods for records generated
or received by the organisation in the course of client engagements,
vendor relationships, and internal operations. It applies to all business
units and to all document types unless a longer retention period is
required by an applicable engagement agreement or by law in the
governing jurisdiction.

2. SCOPE

This policy applies to all records in physical or electronic form,
including but not limited to contracts, correspondence, compliance
reports, checklists, and reviewer notes.

--- page 2 ---

3. STANDARD RETENTION SCHEDULE

3.1 Executed contracts and amendments: retained for seven (7) years
following the later of contract expiration or final obligation
discharge.

3.2 Compliance reports and audit records: retained for five (5) years
following the report date.

3.3 Routine correspondence: retained for three (3) years following the
date of the correspondence, unless the correspondence is incorporated by
reference into a contract or compliance record, in which case the
retention period of the referencing record applies.

3.4 Internal reviewer notes: retained for five (5) years following the
date of the note.

4. DISPOSAL

Records reaching the end of their retention period shall be securely
disposed of in accordance with the organisation's standard data
destruction procedures. Disposal shall be logged and the log retained
for two (2) years.

--- page 3 ---

5. EXCEPTIONS

No exceptions to this policy are currently on file. Any request for an
exception must be submitted in writing to Review Operations and approved
by an authorised reviewer prior to the applicable retention period
lapsing.

6. REVIEW CYCLE

This policy is reviewed annually. The next scheduled review is
January 2027.
