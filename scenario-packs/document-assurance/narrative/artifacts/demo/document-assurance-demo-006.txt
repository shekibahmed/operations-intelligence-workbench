REVIEWER NOTE

Reviewer: Nadia Okonkwo, Review Owner, Review Operations
Date: May 3, 2026
Subject: Project Falcon - liability cap discrepancy identified

While cross-checking the Project Falcon Master Services Agreement
against our internal Data Handling Policy for the onboarding item that
was still outstanding, I found a discrepancy that needs to be tracked
as a conflicting-provisions issue rather than a simple documentation
gap.

MSA Clause 8.2 (ref VLP-FAL-2026-0142) caps aggregate liability under
the agreement, including liability arising from data security
incidents, at two million dollars ($2,000,000).

Data Handling Policy POL-DH-009, Section 4.1, separately states that
liability for security incidents affecting data processed under an
engagement shall not exceed five million dollars ($5,000,000).

These two figures directly conflict with respect to security-incident
liability for Project Falcon. Clause 12.1 of the MSA states that the
Agreement governs over internal policy documents in the event of a
conflict, which would suggest the $2,000,000 figure in Clause 8.2
controls. However, Section 5.1 of the policy frames the relationship the
other way, deferring back to "the precedence terms of the applicable
agreement" without resolving which figure is meant to apply in practice.

I am flagging this as unresolved. This should not be treated as settled
by either document in isolation. I'll be raising this with Vantage's
legal contact for clarification before we treat either figure as
authoritative.

- Nadia
