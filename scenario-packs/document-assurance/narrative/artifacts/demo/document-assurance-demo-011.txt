REVIEWER NOTE - FORMAL EXCEPTION PROPOSAL

Reviewer: Nadia Okonkwo, Review Owner, Review Operations
Date: July 8, 2026
Subject: Project Falcon - proposed "accept exception" decision for
liability cap conflicting-provisions issue

Following the Q2 2026 compliance report (issued July 2, 2026), I am
formally proposing that Review Operations accept an exception with
respect to the unresolved liability cap conflict between MSA Clause 8.2
(ref VLP-FAL-2026-0142) and Data Handling Policy POL-DH-009, Section
4.1, for Project Falcon.

BACKGROUND

Clause 8.2 sets an aggregate liability cap, including for security
incidents, of $2,000,000. Section 4.1 of POL-DH-009 separately sets a
liability standard of $5,000,000 for security incidents. Despite
outreach to Vantage's legal contact (Priya Deshmukh) on May 5 and
May 8, 2026, Vantage's confirmation that "the contract governs" did not
resolve which figure applies in practice, and no further written
clarification has been received as of this note.

PROPOSED EXCEPTION AND CONDITIONS

I propose that Review Operations proceed on the basis that Clause 8.2's
$2,000,000 figure governs for Project Falcon specifically, consistent
with the MSA's own precedence clause (12.1), subject to the following
conditions:

1. This exception applies only to Project Falcon and does not set a
   precedent for other engagements referencing POL-DH-009.
2. The exception is to be reviewed again at the next annual policy
   review cycle (January 2027) or sooner if Vantage provides written
   confirmation resolving the ambiguity.
3. The outstanding insurance certificate item is NOT covered by this
   exception and must continue to be pursued separately as a collection
   item.

This exception, given the liability exposure involved, requires review
and approval by an authorised reviewer before it is treated as accepted.
