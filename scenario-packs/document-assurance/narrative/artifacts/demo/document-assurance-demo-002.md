INTERNAL POLICY DOCUMENT

Data Handling Policy
Policy No. POL-DH-009
Version 2.4
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Owner: Review Operations

1. PURPOSE

This policy establishes standards for the handling of client and vendor
data across all engagements, including engagements involving logistics
partners, insurers, and compliance vendors. It is intended to be
referenced by, and to operate alongside, individual engagement
agreements.

2. SCOPE

This policy applies to all personnel and, where an engagement agreement
references this policy for operational guidance, to the counterparty
under that agreement with respect to data handling practices.

--- page 2 ---

3. DATA SECURITY REQUIREMENTS

3.1 All parties handling data under an active engagement shall implement
reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards
appropriate to the sensitivity of the data involved.

3.2 Any suspected or confirmed security incident affecting data
processed under an engagement shall be reported to Review Operations
within forty-eight (48) hours of discovery.

--- page 3 ---

4. LIABILITY FOR SECURITY INCIDENTS

4.1 Where a security incident results in loss, unauthorised disclosure,
or corruption of data processed under an engagement, the aggregate
liability of the party responsible for the incident, arising out of or
in connection with that incident, shall not exceed five million dollars
($5,000,000).

4.2 This liability standard reflects the organisation's current
assessment of exposure associated with data security incidents across
active engagements and is intended to apply uniformly unless a specific
engagement agreement expressly and unambiguously provides otherwise.

--- page 4 ---

5. APPLICABILITY TO EXISTING AGREEMENTS

5.1 This policy is intended for operational guidance and does not, by
itself, amend the terms of any executed engagement agreement. Where an
engagement agreement contains its own liability provisions, the
relationship between this policy and those provisions is governed by
the precedence terms of the applicable agreement.

6. REVIEW CYCLE

This policy is reviewed annually. The next scheduled review is
January 2027.
