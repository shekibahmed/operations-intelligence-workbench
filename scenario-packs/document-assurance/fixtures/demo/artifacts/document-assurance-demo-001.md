MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

"Project Falcon"
Reference No. VLP-FAL-2026-0142

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of
January 15, 2026, with an effective date of February 1, 2026
("Effective Date"), by and between the organisation ("Review
Operations") and Vantage Logistics Partners, a logistics services
provider organised under the laws of the State of Ashford ("Vantage").

This Agreement governs the engagement internally referred to as
"Project Falcon" and sets out the obligations of both parties in
connection with the logistics review and compliance monitoring
services described herein.

CLAUSE 5. COMPLIANCE REPORTING

5.1 Vantage shall provide, and Review Operations shall review, a
quarterly compliance report addressing the operational and regulatory
status of the logistics services performed under this Agreement.

5.3 Each quarterly compliance report shall be delivered no later than
thirty (30) days following the end of the applicable calendar quarter.
The first such report shall cover the period ending March 31, 2026, and
shall be due no later than April 30, 2026.

CLAUSE 6. INSURANCE

6.1 Vantage shall maintain, at its own expense, commercial general
liability and cargo insurance coverage in amounts consistent with
industry standards for logistics providers operating in the State of
Ashford.

6.2 Vantage shall provide Review Operations with a current certificate
of insurance evidencing such coverage. The initial certificate shall be
provided no later than March 1, 2026, and shall be renewed and
re-submitted annually thereafter on or before each anniversary of that
date.

CLAUSE 8. LIABILITY

8.1 Except as set out in Clause 8.2, each party shall be liable to the
other for direct damages arising from its breach of this Agreement,
subject to the limitations set out in this Clause 8.

8.2 Notwithstanding any other provision of this Agreement, the
aggregate liability of either party arising out of or in connection
with this Agreement, whether in contract, tort, or otherwise, shall not
exceed two million dollars ($2,000,000). This cap applies to all claims
arising under this Agreement, including but not limited to claims
arising from data security incidents affecting information processed
in connection with the services.

8.3 Nothing in this Clause 8 shall limit either party's liability for
gross negligence, wilful misconduct, or fraud.

CLAUSE 12. PRECEDENCE

12.1 In the event of a conflict between the terms of this Agreement and
any internal policy document of either party, including any policy
referenced or incorporated for operational guidance, the terms of this
Agreement shall govern with respect to the rights and obligations of
the parties to each other.

CLAUSE 14. GOVERNING LAW

14.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance
with the laws of the State of Ashford.
