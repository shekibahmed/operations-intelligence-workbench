AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

"Project Falcon"
Reference No. VLP-FAL-2026-0142-A1
(Amending Reference No. VLP-FAL-2026-0142)

This Amendment No. 1 ("Amendment") is entered into as of May 12, 2026,
by and between the organisation ("Review Operations") and Vantage
Logistics Partners ("Vantage"), and amends the Master Services Agreement
referenced above (the "Agreement").

RECITALS

WHEREAS Vantage's insurance broker required additional time to
consolidate two existing policies into a single combined policy prior
to issuing the certificate of insurance required under Clause 6.2 of the
Agreement;

WHEREAS the parties wish to adjust the due date for the initial
certificate of insurance to accommodate this consolidation without
otherwise altering the parties' obligations under the Agreement;

--- page 2 ---

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:

1. AMENDMENT TO CLAUSE 6.2

Clause 6.2 of the Agreement is amended to replace the initial insurance
certificate due date of "March 1, 2026" with "May 1, 2026." All other
terms of Clause 6.2, including the requirement for annual renewal and
re-submission on or before each subsequent anniversary of the certificate
date, remain unchanged. For clarity, the annual renewal cycle going
forward shall be measured from the May 1, 2026 certificate date, not
from the original March 1, 2026 date referenced in the Agreement prior
to this Amendment.

2. NO OTHER CHANGES

Except as expressly amended herein, all other terms and conditions of
the Agreement, including Clause 8.2 and Clause 12.1, remain in full
force and effect and are not modified by this Amendment.
