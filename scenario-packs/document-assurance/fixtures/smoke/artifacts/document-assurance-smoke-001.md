MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

Reference No. MCS-NDA-2026-0007

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of
January 10, 2026 ("Effective Date") by and between the organisation
("Review Operations") and Meridian Compliance Services, a compliance
auditing firm organised under the laws of the State of Ashford
("Meridian").

WHEREAS the parties wish to explore a potential auditing engagement and,
in connection therewith, may disclose to one another certain confidential
and proprietary information;

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:

1. DEFINITION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

"Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by
either party to the other, whether orally or in writing, that is
designated as confidential or that reasonably should be understood to be
confidential given the nature of the information and the circumstances
of disclosure.

--- page 2 ---

2. OBLIGATION OF CONFIDENTIALITY

Each party agrees to hold the other party's Confidential Information in
strict confidence and not to disclose such Confidential Information to
any third party without the prior written consent of the disclosing
party. Each party shall use the Confidential Information solely for the
purpose of evaluating and, if applicable, performing the contemplated
auditing engagement.

3. TERM

This Agreement shall remain in effect for a period of two (2) years from
the Effective Date, after which the confidentiality obligations set out
in Section 2 shall automatically expire unless earlier terminated by
mutual written consent of the parties.

4. GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the
laws of the State of Ashford, without regard to its conflict of laws
principles.

5. NO OBLIGATION TO CONTRACT

Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed as obligating either party
to enter into any further agreement or business relationship.
