Findings:

Brake assembly tested through full range, pedal travel within spec, no
grabbing or spongy feel. Hydraulic lift operates smoothly through full
mast extension with no drift under load. Drive motor and steering
responsive, no play in the wheel. Tires show normal wear for age, forks
straight with no visible cracking at the heel. Overhead guard and
seatbelt intact and functioning. Horn, lights, and backup alarm all
tested and working.

Unit passed inspection with no defects requiring corrective action.

One advisory note for the file: brake pad thickness is within spec but
on the lower end of the normal range for a unit of this age. Recommend
the technician keep an eye on pad wear at the next scheduled service
interval - not a defect at this time, just something to monitor going
forward.

Recommendation: Unit cleared for continued service. Next annual
inspection due approximately 2027-07-01.
