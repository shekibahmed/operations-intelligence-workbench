NORTHGATE FACILITIES
Follow-Up Equipment Inspection Report

Asset: A-301 (Corvale DL-15 hydraulic dock leveler)
Location: Zone A - Receiving, bay 3
Inspection Date: 2026-02-11
Inspector: Lena Fischer, Northgate Facilities

Purpose: Follow-up on hydraulic fluid leak first reported 2026-02-05,
temporarily patched at the cylinder seal on 2026-02-06.

--- page 2 ---

Findings:

The seal patch applied on 2026-02-06 is holding for now - no active
leaking observed during this visit and the shop floor around the
cylinder is clean. That said, this is a patch on an original hydraulic
lift cylinder and should not be treated as a permanent fix. Seal patches
on a cylinder of this age (installed 2020-05-15) tend to hold for weeks
to a few months at best before the weep returns, often worse than
before.

Recommend scheduling a full hydraulic cylinder replacement within 30
days rather than waiting for the patch to fail and the leak to return,
which would likely happen with less warning next time.

Separately: A-302 (bay 4) is the same Corvale DL-15 model, installed the
same date as A-301 (2020-05-15), and has not shown any leak symptoms.
Given that A-301's cylinder is reaching end of life at this age, A-302 is
due for a preventive inspection of its own hydraulic cylinder condition
so we're not caught off guard by the same failure mode there.

--- page 3 ---

Recommendation:

Schedule full hydraulic cylinder replacement on A-301 within 30 days
(by approximately 2026-03-13). Schedule a preventive hydraulic cylinder
inspection on A-302 in the same window.
