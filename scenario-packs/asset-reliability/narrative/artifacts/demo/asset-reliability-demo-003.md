NORTHGATE FACILITIES
Follow-Up Equipment Inspection Report

Asset: A-142 (Talbrook TF-40 electric forklift)
Location: Zone C - Shipping
Inspection Date: 2026-05-21
Inspector: Lena Fischer, Northgate Facilities

Purpose: Follow-up inspection prompted by operator-reported brake grinding
noise and spongy pedal feel (reported 2026-05-18).

Background:
Unit had a full front and rear brake pad replacement performed by Ferrow
Industrial Services on 2026-04-06 (work order FIS-4471) following a
similar soft-pedal complaint at that time. This inspection is intended to
determine whether the current complaint represents a recurrence or a
separate issue.

--- page 2 ---

Findings:

Brake pedal travel is noticeably longer than spec before engagement,
consistent with the operator's description. Some grinding is audible
during hard braking, most pronounced at low speed. Visual inspection of
the brake assembly shows the recently replaced pads are wearing unevenly
- more material loss on the inboard side than expected for six weeks of
service. There is also a small amount of fluid weeping visible at a
fitting on the hydraulic brake line near the left rear wheel, not enough
to call it an active leak yet, but enough to note.

Given that this is the second brake complaint on this unit in under two
months, and the pad wear pattern is not consistent with normal use, this
looks more like a recurring issue than an isolated one - possibly a
sticking caliper or a slow line leak causing uneven pad wear and reduced
pedal firmness. Recommend a more thorough diagnostic beyond a simple pad
swap.

--- page 3 ---

Recommendation:

Follow-up diagnostic and repair should be scheduled within 5 business
days (by 2026-05-26) given the safety-relevant nature of a braking
system and the recurrence pattern. Unit may continue in service in the
interim only with caution and should be pulled immediately if the
operator reports any loss of braking effectiveness, not just noise or
feel.
