NORTHGATE FACILITIES
Follow-Up Equipment Inspection Report

Asset: A-520 (Arkwell AC-60 refrigerant compressor)
Location: Maintenance Bay
Inspection Date: 2026-07-10
Inspector: Lena Fischer, Northgate Facilities

Purpose: Follow-up after repeated breaker trips (three occurrences the
week of 2026-07-06) and a temporary refrigerant top-up performed
2026-07-06.

--- page 2 ---

Findings:

Refrigerant charge is already reading slightly below the level it was
topped up to just four days ago, which points toward a slow leak rather
than a one-time low-charge condition. Repeated breaker trips are
consistent with the compressor motor working harder than it should to
compensate for reduced refrigerant pressure. No obvious major leak point
found on visual inspection, but a proper leak test with the right
equipment is needed to locate it precisely - suspect a slow leak at one
of the line fittings rather than a failed seal, based on the gradual
rather than sudden pressure drop.

Worth noting: A-520 is part of the same 2021 mechanical equipment
install batch as A-501 (Yard backup generator), both installed
2021-02-01 as part of the same facility mechanical upgrade project. This
is the second unit from that install batch to show a slow fluid-loss
pattern at a fitting or seal this year - A-501's cooling system was
flagged for a similar slow loss in April. Not the same fluid or system,
but worth tracking as a pattern across that install batch's fittings and
seals as they age.

--- page 3 ---

Recommendation:

Schedule a proper refrigerant leak test to locate the leak source.
Continue monitoring breaker trip frequency until root cause is
addressed. Given the install-batch pattern, consider a broader fitting
and seal check across other equipment from the 2021-02-01 mechanical
batch.
