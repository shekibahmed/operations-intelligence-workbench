NORTHGATE FACILITIES
Follow-Up Equipment Inspection Report

Asset: A-210 (Meridian CB-200 belt conveyor)
Location: Zone B - Storage High-Bay, induction line 1
Inspection Date: 2026-03-16
Inspector: Lena Fischer, Northgate Facilities

Purpose: Follow-up check after belt tension adjustment performed
2026-03-10 in response to reported slippage under load.

--- page 2 ---

Findings:

Tension adjustment from the prior service is holding, belt is not
currently slipping under test load. However, closer inspection of the
belt surface shows wear consistent with age - some glazing along the
running surface and light fraying starting at one edge. Wear is not yet
severe enough to require immediate replacement, but it is approaching
the point where tension adjustments alone will stop being an effective
fix. My estimate is this belt has a limited remaining service life if
loads and run time stay at current levels.

A-211, the adjacent induction line, was installed in the same batch as
A-210 (2021-06-01) and runs a comparable load and duty cycle. Given that
A-210 is now showing age-related wear, I'd recommend a preventive check
on A-211's belt condition as well, even though it has not had any
reported issues - no need to wait for it to start slipping before we
look.

--- page 3 ---

Recommendation:

Continue monitoring A-210 belt condition at each PM visit. Budget for a
belt replacement in the near term rather than waiting for failure.
Schedule a preventive belt-wear check on A-211 within the next
maintenance cycle.

Inspector signature: Lena Fischer
