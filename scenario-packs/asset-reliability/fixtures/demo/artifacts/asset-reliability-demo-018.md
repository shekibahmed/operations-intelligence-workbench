NORTHGATE FACILITIES
Follow-Up Equipment Inspection Report

Asset: A-501 (Sterling GEN-150 diesel backup generator)
Location: Yard
Inspection Date: 2026-04-29
Inspector: Lena Fischer, Northgate Facilities

Purpose: Follow-up inspection of cooling system approximately two weeks
after an overheat shutdown on 2026-04-14, which was logged as resolved
on 2026-04-15 after a coolant top-up.

--- page 2 ---

Findings:

Coolant level in the reservoir is low again today, back down close to
where it reportedly was before the 2026-04-15 top-up. This is not
consistent with a simple one-time low-fluid event - a unit that was
correctly topped up two weeks ago and has no active leak should not be
back down to a low level already at this point, given normal monthly
test-run usage.

On closer inspection of the radiator, several fins along the lower left
section are bent and partially blocked with yard debris (leaves, dust
buildup packed into the fin gaps). This would reduce cooling airflow and
could explain both the original overheat event and the recurring low
coolant reading, if heat is causing the system to lose fluid through the
overflow/expansion path faster than expected rather than through a
visible ground leak.

This finding directly contradicts the prior repair note that logged this
issue as resolved and returned the unit to full operational status. The
underlying cooling problem does not appear to have been fixed - it looks
like the top-up masked the symptom temporarily rather than addressing
the cause.

--- page 3 ---

Recommendation:

Do not rely on this unit's current "operational" status for cooling
performance under sustained load. Recommend radiator fin cleaning and
straightening, and a pressure test of the cooling system to check for a
slow loss path, before the next scheduled backup-power test. Given this
unit's role as backup power, recommend flagging for review ahead of any
facility power-reliability audit.

Inspector signature: Lena Fischer
