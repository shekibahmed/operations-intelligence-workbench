Repair Update - Zone A / Bay 3

Asset: A-301 (Corvale DL-15 hydraulic dock leveler)
Date: 2026-02-06
Logged by: Marcus Ibe

Followed up on Dana's report of fluid under the leveler at bay 3. Found
the source - a small weep at the seal on the main hydraulic lift
cylinder, not a hose or fitting like I expected. Cleaned up the spill and
applied a seal patch/sealant compound to slow the weep since we don't
have a replacement cylinder in stock right now.

This is a temporary measure, not a real fix. Patched seal should hold for
a while but I would not consider this resolved - the cylinder itself is
original to the unit and this kind of weep usually means it's getting
close to end of useful life. Cycled the leveler a dozen times after the
patch, no visible leaking during that test, but keeping an eye on it.

Status: A-301 remains in service. Flagging for inspection follow-up to
assess whether a full cylinder replacement is needed.
