--- page 2 ---

Findings:

Noticed something on the overhead guard frame that concerns me - looks
like it could be a crack near one of the upright supports, hard to tell
for certain without getting better light on it or pulling out proper
measuring tools, which I did not have on hand during this pass. Didn't
have the camera with me either so there's no photo of it in this report.
It could be a crack, or it could be old paint wear or a surface scratch
catching the light a certain way - I honestly can't say for sure from
this look.

Given that this is a structural component and the consequence of being
wrong could be serious, I'd rather flag this cautiously now than wait
for a proper look later.

--- page 3 ---

Recommendation:

Out of caution, recommending this unit be considered for immediate
removal from service until someone can get proper measurements and
photos of the area in question. I want to stress that I do not have
solid confirmation this is an actual structural crack - this is a
flag based on a quick visual pass, not a confirmed defect. A follow-up
inspection with proper tools and documentation should happen before any
final decision is made either way.
